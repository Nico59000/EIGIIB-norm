# E14-A3 manual review

Status: complete.

The review confirms that:

- E14-A3 consumes exact E14-A1 and E14-A2 identities without re-proving their semantic claims;
- only prior committed consumptions reduce budgets;
- authorization denial, revoked control state and exhausted budget have negative precedence;
- held and unavailable remain distinct;
- isolated, pairwise and declared-shared modes have separate replay rules;
- operation-nonce, projection, source-record and scoped-budget limits are independently checked;
- a committed static ledger entry is not described as distributed atomic execution;
- anonymity, unlinkability, erasure, audience authentication, release and revocation freshness remain outside this slice.
