# E16-A2 Manual Review

Status: complete.

The review confirms that:

- E16-A2 starts from exact historical replay of E16-A1 head `7fd50a2009c6a437c7fe0b680407cf337b55cf4f`;
- a placement request is not placement evidence;
- custody acceptance is not legal ownership transfer or a retention promise;
- failure-domain labels are declarations and do not prove physical separation or provider independence;
- a positive placement observation is bounded to the represented event;
- explicit rejection, negative observation, content mismatch and reference substitution take precedence over held or unavailable states;
- retention, independent readback and restore verification remain reserved for E16-A3;
- no live provider credential, secret, endpoint token or customer locator is present in the normative registry.
