# EIGIIB E1/E2/E3/E4/E5/E6/E7/E8 manual gates

Revision reviewed: `EIGIIB-E8-draft-1.0`.

- `semantic-authority-review`: complete. The EIGIIB core remains authoritative for the general rule; E1 owns typed evidence/claim semantics; E2 owns static repository checking semantics; E3 owns artifact identity, production provenance, replay and reproducibility semantics; E4 owns authenticated attestations, trust roots, delegation, revocation and provenance-integrity semantics; E5 owns transparency logs, append-only checkpoint relations, witnessing and transparent trust-history semantics; E6 owns multi-observer gossip comparison, cross-log anchoring and bounded fork-accountability semantics; E7 owns recovery actions, remediation plans, monotone trust-state transitions, rollback, continuity and bounded incident closure semantics; E8 owns relying-party adoption observations, migration coverage, compatibility windows, explicit exceptions and bounded cutover semantics.
- `claim-boundary-review`: complete. The E2 reference checker limits its claim to mechanically decidable repository properties and does not claim semantic validation of manual attestations.
- `provenance-boundary-review`: complete. The E3 model keeps byte identity, provenance and reproducibility distinct from authenticity. The selected E3 closure covers only stable E3 normative/schema/checker/test artifacts; mutable cross-extension evidence is not part of that immutable closure.
- `authentication-boundary-review`: complete. E4 separates cryptographic signature validity, trust-root acceptance, authorization, temporal/revocation policy and semantic truth. The repository's `conformance/trust.json` remains structural-only and asserts no production trust root or authenticated production decision.
- `transparency-boundary-review`: complete. E5 separates log inclusion, append-only consistency, witness coverage, E4 authentication and semantic truth. The repository's `conformance/transparency.json` is structural-only and asserts no production transparency log, production checkpoint, production witness, witness independence, fork-free global view, or complete transparent trust history.
- `gossip-accountability-boundary-review`: complete. E6 separates received views, E4-authenticated views, E5 consistency references, direct conflicts, cross-log commitments, attribution and remediation. The repository's `conformance/gossip.json` is structural-only and asserts no live gossip network, production peer independence, cross-log federation, observed production fork, culpability, real-world identity, malicious intent, legal responsibility, or atomic cross-log state.
- `recovery-continuity-boundary-review`: complete. E7 separates incident detection, containment, remediation, trust-state transition, continuity, rollback, closure and root-cause resolution. The repository's `conformance/recovery.json` is structural-only: it asserts no production incident, completed production remediation, restored production trust state, successful production rollback, or closed production incident.
- `relying-party-convergence-boundary-review`: complete. E8 separates E7 continuity, new-state acceptance, old-state rejection, migration coverage, compatibility, exceptions and cutover. The repository's `conformance/convergence.json` is structural-only: it asserts no production relying-party inventory, production adoption observation, fleet convergence, legacy-rejection completion or production cutover.

The E7 baseline checker executes no recovery action and mutates no trust store. Its hardening profile 0.2 adds incident-boundary coherence, lower-layer binding checks, rollback-replacement epoch checks and guarded positive-result counting; it remains static and does not strengthen external facts.

The E8 reference checker contacts no relying party and mutates no deployment. It treats `domain` and `class` only as declared counting labels, never as proof of independence. Positive adoption requires evidence, and `converged-with-exceptions` exposes rather than hides uncovered required parties. New-state acceptance is kept separate from rejection of the superseded state.

Post-cutover stale acceptance remains a typed observation. It is not automatically a structural failure and MUST NOT be rewritten as compromise, malicious intent or attribution. A convergence or cutover decision becomes non-conformant only when it overclaims relative to its declared policy and observations.

E4 remains authoritative for authentication, E5 for append-only/witness semantics, E6 for gossip/fork accountability, and E7 for recovery/trust-state continuity. E8 may consume E7 continuity decisions but does not re-prove them.

Convergence is explicitly closure-bounded. A satisfied quorum or complete set of declared required parties does not prove global convergence of unknown, offline, cached or unregistered relying parties.

No deviation is accepted by this attestation.
