# EIGIIB E1/E2/E3/E4/E5/E6/E7 manual gates

Revision reviewed: `EIGIIB-E7-draft-1.0`.

- `semantic-authority-review`: complete. The EIGIIB core remains authoritative for the general rule; E1 owns typed evidence/claim semantics; E2 owns static repository checking semantics; E3 owns artifact identity, production provenance, replay and reproducibility semantics; E4 owns authenticated attestations, trust roots, delegation, revocation and provenance-integrity semantics; E5 owns transparency logs, append-only checkpoint relations, witnessing and transparent trust-history semantics; E6 owns multi-observer gossip comparison, cross-log anchoring and bounded fork-accountability semantics; E7 owns recovery actions, remediation plans, monotone trust-state transitions, rollback, continuity and bounded incident closure semantics.
- `claim-boundary-review`: complete. The E2 reference checker limits its claim to mechanically decidable repository properties and does not claim semantic validation of manual attestations.
- `provenance-boundary-review`: complete. The E3 model keeps byte identity, provenance and reproducibility distinct from authenticity. The selected E3 closure covers only stable E3 normative/schema/checker/test artifacts; mutable cross-extension evidence is not part of that immutable closure.
- `authentication-boundary-review`: complete. E4 separates cryptographic signature validity, trust-root acceptance, authorization, temporal/revocation policy and semantic truth. The repository's `conformance/trust.json` remains structural-only and asserts no production trust root or authenticated production decision.
- `transparency-boundary-review`: complete. E5 separates log inclusion, append-only consistency, witness coverage, E4 authentication and semantic truth. The repository's `conformance/transparency.json` is structural-only and asserts no production transparency log, production checkpoint, production witness, witness independence, fork-free global view, or complete transparent trust history.
- `gossip-accountability-boundary-review`: complete. E6 separates received views, E4-authenticated views, E5 consistency references, direct conflicts, cross-log commitments, attribution and remediation. The repository's `conformance/gossip.json` is structural-only and asserts no live gossip network, production peer independence, cross-log federation, observed production fork, culpability, real-world identity, malicious intent, legal responsibility, or atomic cross-log state.
- `recovery-continuity-boundary-review`: complete. E7 separates incident detection, containment, remediation, trust-state transition, continuity, rollback, closure and root-cause resolution. The repository's `conformance/recovery.json` is structural-only: it asserts no production incident, completed production remediation, restored production trust state, successful production rollback, or closed production incident.

The E7 reference checker executes no recovery action and mutates no trust store. A completed E7 action requires evidence, but the checker does not infer that all external relying parties adopted the intended effect. Verified recovery transitions strictly advance logical trust-state epochs; rollback is represented as a later compensating transition and never as history deletion or epoch rewind.

E4 remains authoritative for authentication, E5 for append-only/witness semantics, and E6 for gossip/fork accountability. E7 may consume their typed decisions but may not strengthen them. In particular, E7 recovery responding to `single-principal-equivocation` does not convert that bounded E6 conclusion into malicious intent, real-world identity, or legal responsibility.

Incident closure is explicitly bounded and reopenable. `closed` does not mean global safety, complete causal understanding, or absence of latent recurrence risk.

No deviation is accepted by this attestation.
