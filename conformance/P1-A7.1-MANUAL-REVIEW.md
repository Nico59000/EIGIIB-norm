# P1-A7.1 schema, taxonomy and generator boundary review

Revision reviewed: `EIGIIB-P1-A7.1-1.0`.

- `p1-negative-corpus-structural-boundary-review`: complete.
- The negative-vector schema, portable error taxonomy and deterministic mutation generator are separate authoritative artifacts.
- Every taxonomy class has one unique contiguous precedence rank.
- Multi-defect precedence is declared by vector and checked against the taxonomy.
- Every source and generated output is bound by SHA-256 and byte length.
- Repository-relative source paths are confined and symlinks are rejected.
- Generator conformance does not imply that any P1 route has executed or rejected a vector.
- Declared expected classes are future route obligations and are not represented as observed equivalence.
- The initial seed corpus is a generator self-test and does not claim production-input coverage.
- P1-A7.1 does not replace P1-A1 through P1-A6 or any numbered `E*` authority.

No deviation is accepted by this attestation.
