# M0-A13 — Post-Freeze Maintenance, Reopening Authority and Supersession Governance

M0-A13 defines how a genuinely frozen M0 lineage may be maintained without mutating its history. The original freeze `eigiib-m0-final-freeze-v1` remains immutable. A normative change is represented by a bounded reopening event, an exact scope, three-college convergence, a successor authority, an append-only supersession record and a fresh independent refreeze readback.

The repository baseline contains no final M0 freeze and no maintenance evidence. It therefore remains `NF`. Even a future positive decision is limited to one registered maintenance cycle.
