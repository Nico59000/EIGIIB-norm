# M0-A14 — Multi-Cycle Maintenance Continuity, Reopening Revocation and Governance Drift Control

M0-A14 extends the one-cycle M0-A13 authority into a bounded append-only history. It verifies cycle sequencing, refreeze digest continuity, effective reopening revocation and governance snapshots without claiming future stability.

A positive decision requires M0-A13 `T`, at least three exact cycles over at least thirty days, one valid revocation exercise, no write at or after revocation, and no undeclared or weakening governance drift. Every changed governance snapshot requires a three-college, independently reviewed transition.

The baseline contains no evidence and remains `NF`. No reopening authority, governance approval, maintenance execution, revocation or refreeze is created by this tranche.
