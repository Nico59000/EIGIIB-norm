2. Classify the maintenance request and enumerate every affected path and claim; wildcards are forbidden.
3. Create the maintenance plan and rollback plan without changing frozen refs.
4. Obtain 4-of-5 approvals in each of the normative, operational and independent-verification colleges, with distinct control domains and one identical request digest.
5. Reject execution after expiry or revocation.
6. Implement under a new `M0-R<n>` successor authority; never force-update the frozen heads.
7. Independently replay the exact approved scope and all inherited conformance suites.
8. Publish the append-only supersession record and reject stale predecessor authority.
9. Produce a new refreeze manifest, all-green exact-head workflow inventory and independent published readback.
10. Close the event with a maintenance certificate. A later change requires a new reopening event.
