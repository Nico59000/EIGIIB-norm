# E16-A5 Human Mastery Guide

## Review order

1. Confirm exact ancestry from the frozen E16-A4 head.
2. Read the A5 transition and verify that A1–A4 are replayed rather than rewritten.
3. Inspect the frozen matrix and both verifier implementations.
4. Confirm separate-process execution and byte-identical canonical reports.
5. Inspect the final closure authority and the complete 95-path freeze.
6. Confirm the stable profile, graph binding and final manual gate.

## Positive interpretation

`e16-preservation-closure-verified` means that every registered verifier route agreed on the exact frozen content identity and generation for the represented corpus case. It does not mean that all possible verifiers, providers or future restore operations agree.

## Negative interpretation

A rejected vector is decisive even when another input is held or unavailable. Loss or quarantine of the successor target, a stale generation, content divergence, route duplication or a non-conformant final freeze prevents closure.

## Independence boundary

Different source files, processes, route identifiers and declared domains establish registered separation only. Reviewers must not infer real administrative, organizational, hardware or provider independence.

## Final freeze

The freeze is a repository identity boundary. It does not create external durability or prevent later repository mutation. Any descendant must preserve this closure through an explicit historical transition.
