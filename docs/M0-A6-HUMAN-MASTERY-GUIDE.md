## Interpretation discipline

A transport or adapter may preserve some properties and not others. Record the source type, target type, policy, context and lost guarantees before interpreting its result.

Equal digests prove equality only for the declared byte object. They do not make an endpoint, recipient, registry, acknowledgement or withdrawal authority interchangeable.

## Stop conditions

Stop E15 entry if:

- the E14 source commit differs;
- an E14 frozen path changes;
- E15 is adopted before the transition bridge exists;
- one local observation is used to claim global delivery or durability;
- a composite score is introduced without declared precedence and normalization.
